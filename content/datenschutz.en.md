## 2. Definitions and legal basis

**Personal data** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. **Processing** covers any operation performed on personal data — collecting, storing, using, disclosing, deleting.

We process personal data in accordance with Swiss data-protection law (revDSG, DSV) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 3. Type, scope, and purpose

We process the personal data needed to perform our activities reliably, securely, and conveniently. Such data may include master and contact data, browser and device data, content and usage data, and contract data.

We process personal data for the duration required for the respective purpose or by law. Personal data no longer required is anonymised or deleted.

## 4. Job applications

We process personal data of applicants insofar as it is required to assess suitability for an employment relationship. Documents submitted voluntarily are treated confidentially.

## 5. Cross-border processing

We process personal data primarily in Switzerland. We may transfer personal data to other countries where adequate data protection is ensured or appropriate safeguards (in particular standard contractual clauses) are in place.

## 6. Rights of data subjects

Data subjects have the right to:

- **Information** about the personal data we process
- **Rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure** and **restriction** of processing
- **Objection** to processing
- **Data portability**

To exercise these rights, contact us at [welcome@peakscale.ch](mailto:welcome@peakscale.ch).

The Swiss data-protection supervisory authority is the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC)](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/edoeb/en/home.html).

## 7. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure data security in line with the risk. Access to our website uses transport encryption (TLS / HTTPS).

## 8. Use of the website

### 8.1 Cookies

Our website does **not** set any cookies for marketing or tracking purposes. Technically necessary cookies — if used at all — may be set without explicit consent.

### 8.2 Server log files

When you visit our website, our hosting provider records technical information (IP address, date/time, browser, operating system) in log files. This information is required to provide and secure the website.

## 9. Analytics

We use **Plausible Analytics** (Plausible Insights OÜ, Estonia) for cookieless, privacy-friendly analytics. No personal data is processed, IP addresses are not stored, and no cross-site profiles are built. See [plausible.io/privacy](https://plausible.io/privacy).

## 10. Third-party services

We use services from specialised providers to operate our activities reliably, securely, and conveniently. These include:

- **GitHub Pages** as hosting provider ([GitHub privacy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies))
- **Plausible Analytics** for analytics (see clause 9)
